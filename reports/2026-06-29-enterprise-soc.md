## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

- **Edge device exploitation is the dominant initial access vector.** CVE-2025-0282 (Ivanti Connect
  Secure, pre-auth RCE, CVSS 9.0), CVE-2024-3400 (Palo Alto PAN-OS GlobalProtect, CVSS 10.0), and
  CVE-2024-21762 (Fortinet FortiOS OOB write, CVSS 9.6) were under confirmed mass exploitation at
  the training cutoff. Organizations running unpatched VPN/SSLVPN appliances face near-certain
  compromise. *(Source: CISA KEV, NVD, Mandiant)*

- **Ransomware ecosystem restructured after law-enforcement disruptions.** Following LockBit
  takedown (Feb 2024) and ALPHV/BlackCat exit-scam (Mar 2024), RansomHub emerged as the dominant
  successor; Akira and Black Basta follow closely. All three operate Ransomware-as-a-Service (RaaS)
  affiliate models, lowering barriers to entry and accelerating targeting of healthcare, financial
  services, and critical infrastructure. *(Source: CISA AA24-131A, AA24-109A, FBI Joint Advisory)*

- **Nation-state threat elevated for critical infrastructure operators.** Volt Typhoon (PRC)
  confirmed pre-positioning in U.S. electric grid, water, and telecom via edge-device footholds and
  living-off-the-land (LOTL) techniques; Salt Typhoon's telecom breach scope expanded to at least
  eight U.S. carriers through training cutoff. *(Source: CISA/FBI/NSA AA24-038A, Mandiant,
  Microsoft MSTIC)*

- **AI is compressing time-to-exploit.** Zero Day Clock TTE data through 2024-2025 shows median
  exploit availability for critical edge-device CVEs under 5 days from publication. Organizations
  relying on 30-day patch SLAs are categorically exposed during that window for CISA KEV-listed
  vulnerabilities. *(Source: Zero Day Clock, Mandiant M-Trends 2025)*

- **Supply chain targeting via developer toolchains remains sustained.** XZ Utils backdoor
  (CVE-2024-3094, CVSS 10.0) and Polyfill.io JavaScript compromise demonstrated systematic
  infiltration of developer trust chains; CI/CD pipeline poisoning and malicious npm/PyPI packages
  are an active threat to API and payment system build pipelines. *(Source: NVD, CISA Advisory,
  GitHub Security Advisories)*

- **Infostealer market expanding credential exfiltration.** Lumma, RedLine, and Vidar stealers
  continued flooding underground markets with corporate credential dumps, session tokens, and API
  keys through training cutoff; payment and API credential exposure directly enables follow-on
  fraud and ransomware. *(Source: Recorded Future, Mandiant, Cisco Talos)*

  requested 7-day window. See Intelligence Gaps (§9) and Source Coverage Ledger (Appendix A).

{

rule Webshell_PHP_Generic_PostExploit
{
    meta:
