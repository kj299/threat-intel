  affiliate.

  particularly for phishing lure generation, automated OSINT, and LLM-assisted PoC
  variant derivation from published patches. This materially shortens exploit
  timelines (CISA KEV median time-to-exploit compressing toward days for high-CVSS
  network-reachable vulnerabilities).

  and poisoned open-source dependencies continued to be used for credential theft and
  backdoor implantation; Lazarus Group (DPRK) demonstrated sophisticated supply-chain
  and social-engineering capability.

- **Credential access and living-off-the-land** are the consistently top technique
  clusters across all actor classes. AiTM phishing proxies (Evilginx, Modlishka) are
  bypassing software-TOTP MFA at scale; LSASS dumping (Mimikatz, procdump,
