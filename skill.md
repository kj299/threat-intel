## Configuration

### Environment Variables

```yaml
SENTINEL_X_MODE: "standard"  # quick_scan | standard | deep_research | continuous
SENTINEL_X_PERSONA: "auto"   # auto | enterprise_soc | executive | smb | researcher | individual | red_team
SENTINEL_X_OUTPUT: "auto"    # auto | executive | technical | ioc_package | checklist | dashboard
SENTINEL_X_CACHE_TTL: 60     # minutes
SENTINEL_X_MAX_SOURCES: 150  # limit concurrent source queries
```
